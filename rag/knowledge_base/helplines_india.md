# Verified Mental Health Crisis Resources in India

## National Emergency & Helplines (24/7)

### Tele-MANAS (National Tele Mental Health Programme)
- **Helpline Numbers**: `14416` or `1800-891-4416` (Toll-Free, 24/7)
- **Authority**: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India
- **Languages**: 20+ Indian regional languages and English
- **Description**: Comprehensive mental health counseling, crisis de-escalation, and psychiatric referral linkages across all States and Union Territories.

### National Emergency Services
- **Helpline Number**: `112` (Toll-Free, 24/7)
- **Services**: Police, Ambulance, Fire, Disaster Response

### KIRAN Mental Health Helpline
- **Helpline Number**: `1800-599-0019` (Toll-Free, 24/7)
- **Authority**: Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment
- **Focus**: Early screening, psychological first aid, panic management, suicide prevention.

### Vandrevala Foundation for Mental Health
- **Helpline Number**: `+91 9999 666 555` / `1860-266-2345` (24/7)
- **Description**: Free, confidential crisis counseling with trained psychological professionals.

### iCALL Psychosocial Helpline (TISS)
- **Contact**: `9152987821` / `022-25521111`
- **Schedule**: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- **Institution**: Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai
