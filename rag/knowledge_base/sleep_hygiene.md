# Clinical Sleep Hygiene and Circadian Health

## Circadian Biology & Sleep Drive
Human sleep architecture is governed by two complementary processes:
1. **Process S (Homeostatic Sleep Drive)**: The continuous biochemical accumulation of adenosine during wakefulness.
2. **Process C (Circadian Phase)**: The 24-hour rhythmic oscillation driven by the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN), synchronized primarily by photic input (ambient light).

## Evidence-Based Sleep Protocols

### Photic Alignment (Light Management)
- **Morning Sunlight**: 10 to 20 minutes of outdoor natural sunlight exposure within one hour of waking stimulates cortisol release and anchors the nocturnal melatonin onset timer ~14–16 hours later.
- **Evening Light Attenuation**: Blue and high-lux wavelengths suppress pineal melatonin synthesis. Eliminate overhead harsh illumination and screens 60 minutes before scheduled sleep.

### Stimulus Control Therapy (SCT)
Developed by Dr. Richard Bootzin, SCT is the gold-standard behavioral treatment for insomnia:
- Reserve the bed exclusively for sleep and sexual intimacy.
- Do not read, work on laptops, study, or watch television in bed.
- **The 20-Minute Protocol**: If unable to fall asleep or return to sleep within ~20 minutes, exit the bed and bedroom. Sit quietly under low, warm light and engage in relaxing non-screen activities until clear sleepiness emerges.

### Temperature and Environment
- Thermoregulatory drop: The core body temperature must decrease by approximately 1 to 2 degrees Fahrenheit to initiate slow-wave sleep. Recommended ambient room temperature is 18°C–20°C (65°F–68°F).
- Limit caffeine intake within 8 to 10 hours of sleep, as caffeine's biological half-life is 5 to 7 hours, blocking adenosine receptors.
